PHISHING INDICATORS AND RULES

1. URGENT_LANGUAGE
Keywords: urgent, immediately, action required, verify now, suspended, deadline
Score: 1

2. ACCOUNT_WARNING
Keywords: account suspended, unusual activity, security alert, unauthorized login
Score: 1

3. CREDENTIAL_REQUEST
Keywords: password, login details, OTP, PIN, verification code
Score: 2

4. SUSPICIOUS_LINK
Keywords: click here, verify your account, reset your password, login now
Score: 2

5. SENSITIVE_INFORMATION
Keywords: bank details, card number, personal information, payment information
Score: 2

6. UNEXPECTED_ATTACHMENT
Keywords: attachment, invoice, document, zip file, pdf
Score: 1

7. SUSPICIOUS_SENDER
Indicators: unknown sender, unusual email address, mismatched domain
Score: 1

RISK CLASSIFICATION

0-2   = LOW RISK
3-5   = MEDIUM RISK
6+    = HIGH RISK
